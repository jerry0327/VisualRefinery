## Type definitions

- **Fact** — directly supported by the cited source.
- **Synthesis** — combines multiple supported facts into a higher-level conclusion.
- **Inference** — a reasoned interpretation that is not explicitly stated by the source.

## Review prompts

For each load-bearing claim, ask:

1. Is the source authoritative enough for the consequence of the claim?
2. Could this information have changed?
3. Does the artifact preserve the source's scope and conditions?
4. Are units, denominators, populations, dates, and comparison conditions explicit?
5. If sources disagree, has the disagreement been resolved or represented?
6. Does the visual treatment imply more certainty than the evidence supports?
