## Final defects check

Confirm there are no unresolved:

- clipping or truncation;
- overlaps;
- off-canvas objects;
- unreadable citations;
- broken links;
- missing glyphs;
- low-resolution or distorted images;
- insufficient contrast;
- accidental large empty areas;
- misleading chart scales or labels;
- orphaned shapes or template remnants;
- inconsistencies introduced by export.
